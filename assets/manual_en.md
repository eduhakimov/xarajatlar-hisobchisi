# Personal Expense Tracker

## Getting started
1. On the **Accounts & categories** page add your accounts: cash, Humo, Uzcard, dollars and so on.
   Set the currency and the current balance (opening balance) for each.
2. If you have a USD or RUB account, enter the rate on the **Rates & backup** page. The app works offline.

## Add an entry
Press `Ctrl+N` or **New entry**. Choose the type (Expense, Income, Transfer), amount, account and category.
`Enter` saves; `Ctrl+Enter` saves and keeps the window open for the next entry.
For a transfer between currencies, type the received amount or leave it empty to use the rate.

## Transactions
Filter by period, type, account, category and note. Double-click to edit, `Del` to delete.
**Export** saves the filtered list to Excel or CSV.

## Budget
Type a monthly limit for a category and press `Enter`. The bar turns amber above 80% and red over the limit.
**Copy last month** carries the limits over to a new month.

## Debts and recurring payments
**Debts**: who you lent to or borrowed from, the due date and partial repayments. A fully repaid debt is closed.
**Recurring**: rent, internet or salary entries are added automatically on their date.

## Backup
On the **Rates & backup** page, **Create backup** saves all data to one .db file.
**Restore** checks the file, saves a copy of the current data automatically, then replaces it.
